Racks & power

A rack is a governed physical system, not a drawing.

Configure capacity, numbering direction, contracted power, feeds, PDU placement and equipment rules independently for every rack.

  • Top-to-bottom or bottom-to-top RU numbering
  • Zero-U and rack-mounted PDU placement
  • Feed capacity and calculated maximum demand
  • Occupied-range validation and planned placement
  • Front, rear and thermal-map views

Asset management

A complete biography for every physical asset.

OrderedPurchase and configuration recorded

Supplier, cost, warranty, serial identity and exact manufacturer part number.

ReceivedPlaced in Warehouse

Accountable owner, custodian, location and verification evidence.

InstalledRack position and power assigned

Ports are created from the selected model and physical connections become available.

In serviceTasks, moves and maintenance

Every operation remains attached to the asset lifecycle.

DispositionSold, recycled or archived

The asset is never silently deleted; its full history remains available.
